## A new place for HumemAI updates

We have published the first video on our YouTube channel. This marks the start of a public video stream where we can explain what we are building, how our research connects to products, and what we learn as the company evolves.

The opening video is intentionally simple: it introduces HumemAI, outlines the direction behind our work on AI systems with human-like memory, and gives viewers a clear place to start if they are new to the company.

## What to expect next

Future videos will cover research releases, open-source projects, infrastructure work, and practical perspectives on memory-centric AI systems. If you want the written technical context behind that work, the best next step is to browse the [project pages](/projects), where HumemAI keeps the longer-form explanations.
